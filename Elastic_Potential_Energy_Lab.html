      HOOKE'S LAW

      F = −kx

      Restoring force is proportional to displacement and opposite in direction.

      ELASTIC PE

      U = ½kx²

      Energy stored in a stretched or compressed spring.

      ENERGY CONSERVATION

      U + KE = constant

      In a frictionless spring–mass system, total mechanical energy never changes.
